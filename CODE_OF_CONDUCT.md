# Our community code of conduct

Be kind, curious, and constructive. Help make scheduled family viewing calmer and more thoughtful.

- Respect people of every background and ability. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and exploitation are not welcome.
- GitHub issues and discussions are public. Never post children’s details, credentials, sign-in links, private viewing history, or other personal information.
- Use issues for actionable bugs and feature proposals, and discussions for questions and ideas.
- Respect creators and YouTube’s policies. Do not propose extracting streams, hiding attribution, or interfering with the standard player.
- Contact hello@thedailytelly.com privately for account, privacy, security, or safety concerns.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation. Explain moderation decisions where practical and keep private reports confidential.
