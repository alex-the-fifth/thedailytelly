Read our privacy promise ↗

We ask for an adult’s email address, not children’s names, birthdays, or profiles. Your schedule and saved progress belong to your account. You can export or delete them.

We don’t sell data, run analytics, fingerprint devices, show our own ads, or offer paid upgrades. The code is open source under MIT.

An honest boundary: videos play through YouTube. Its player may show ads and process data under Google’s policies. We cannot promise ad-free or tracking-free YouTube playback, and channel selection does not guarantee child-appropriate content.
