# Code of conduct

The public code is included in /conduct (server/static/policies.html).

- Adult-managed households only; no child profiles or social features.
- Respect children, other people, creators, and shared infrastructure.
- No harassment, exploitation, discrimination, impersonation, illegal use, or attacks.
- No unauthorized pairing, scraping accounts, bypassing controls, or stream extraction.
- Report concerns privately to hello@thedailytelly.com without child details or secrets.
- Accounts can be suspended for safety; operator actions are recorded and questions
  can be raised through the contact address.
- Free, open-source operation with no paid tiers, analytics, fingerprinting, or data sale.

The policy does not certify content as child-appropriate or replace supervision.
