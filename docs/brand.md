# A familiar weekly rhythm

The Daily Telly is about scheduled viewing: familiar favourites on familiar days,
with room for life beyond the screen. Recall the predictability of a few TV
channels without claiming historical broadcast days for particular programmes.

Use warm, plain language. Emphasize parent choice, routines, and boundaries. Do not
promise suitability, ad-free YouTube playback, automated total-screen-time limits,
or platform enforcement that the app does not provide.

The example week is Monday: Sesame Street; Tuesday: Mister Rogers’ Neighborhood;
Wednesday: Bluey; Thursday: SciShow Kids; Friday: Daniel Tiger And Friends;
Saturday: Nat Geo Kids; Sunday: The Magic School Bus. Treat these as examples and
third-party names, without implying endorsement. Avoid character likenesses.

Palette: paper #f6f5ee, ink #183d34, muted #63736c, mint #dcebdc, peach #ee875c,
lavender #e7e1f2. Use serif headlines with readable system sans-serif body text.
Emails use inline styles, local typography, no remote images, and a plain-text
alternative. The television/play mark is an original code-native vector.
